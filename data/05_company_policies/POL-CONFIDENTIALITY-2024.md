# Confidential Information Policy (2024)

## Classification
- **Public** – website, brochures.
- **Internal** – procedures, org charts.
- **Confidential** – client lists, CRM data, pricing, budgets, financial results before publication.
- **Strictly confidential** – M&A, salaries, personal data of special categories.

## Rules
- Confidential information may only be shared on a need-to-know basis.
- It may not be sent to private e-mail accounts or stored on personal devices or accounts.
- Contacts with journalists are handled exclusively by the CEO or Communications.
- Breaches are handled according to the Disciplinary Procedure and may constitute serious cause.

## After employment
The obligation of confidentiality continues after the end of the contract (art. 17, 3° Law of 3 July 1978).
