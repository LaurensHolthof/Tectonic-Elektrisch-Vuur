# Anti-Harassment Procedure (2017)

1. Employees who feel harassed contact **the HR department**, which investigates the complaint.
2. HR hears both parties and decides whether the facts are proven.
3. If proven, HR proposes a sanction to management.
4. An external prevention adviser may be consulted if HR considers it necessary.

*Note (2024): this procedure did not comply with the Law of 4 August 1996 as amended in 2014 and has been replaced.*
