# Sickness Absence Procedure (implements work regulations art. 7-10)

1. **Call or message your manager** before 9:30 on the first day.
2. **Send your medical certificate within 2 working days** from the first day of incapacity (HR portal or hr@tectonic-solutions.be). Certificates issued by doctors in other EU countries are accepted.
3. First-day absence without certificate: allowed twice per calendar year for the first day of an incapacity (art. 31 §2/1 Law of 3 July 1978, companies with 50+ employees).
4. If the certificate is late, payroll may refuse guaranteed salary for the days before the certificate was sent (art. 31 Law 3 July 1978). Discuss with HR before applying this.
5. Control doctor: you must accept a visit or go to the control doctor's practice if invited.
6. Long-term absence (> 4 weeks): HR contacts you about the return-to-work pathway.
