# Home Office Allowance Policy (2025)

- Employees with a structural telework annex receive a monthly **home-office allowance of €150 net** (office costs), plus an internet contribution of €20.
- The allowance is paid only for months in which the employee teleworks structurally from their **registered home address**.
- It is **not paid for periods of international remote work** exceeding 20 working days in a month.
- Equipment: laptop, screen and headset are provided by the company.
- The allowance is indexed each January according to the rules accepted by the tax authorities.
