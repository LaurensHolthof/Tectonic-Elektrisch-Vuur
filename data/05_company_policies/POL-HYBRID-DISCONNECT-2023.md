# Hybrid Work & Disconnection Guidelines (April 2023)

These guidelines explain the company CCT on the right to disconnect of 14 March 2023 in practical terms.

## Principles

- Nobody is expected to read or answer work messages **between 19:00 and 08:00**, during weekends, holidays or leave, except colleagues who are on call (see On-call Policy).
- Managers do not send messages outside working hours unless urgent; use "schedule send".
- Not answering outside working hours can never lead to a negative evaluation or sanction.

## Tools

- Set your Teams status and out-of-office.
- Mobile notifications can be switched off outside your working hours.

## Meetings

- No meetings before 9:00 or after 17:30.
- Wednesday afternoon: meeting-light (focus time).
