# Diversity & Non-Discrimination Policy (March 2024)

Tectonic treats all candidates and employees equally, regardless of gender, pregnancy, maternity, family situation, age, origin, nationality, religion or belief, disability, sexual orientation, health condition or union membership, in line with the Belgian anti-discrimination laws of 10 May 2007.

## Commitments

- Recruitment based on job-related criteria only (CCT n° 38).
- Equal pay for work of equal value (CCT n° 25); annual gender pay-gap analysis.
- **Part-time work, parental leave and other family-related leave never lead to less favourable treatment** in evaluation, pay, bonus or promotion.
- Reasonable accommodation for employees with a disability.

## Reporting

Report concerns to your HR Business Partner, the person of trust or the prevention adviser. External: Unia or the Institute for the Equality of Women and Men.
