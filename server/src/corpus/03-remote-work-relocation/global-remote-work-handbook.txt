Employees are permitted to work remotely from any EU member state for up to 90 consecutive calendar days per fiscal year upon manager approval.

Employees working remotely outside their home country must verify their own personal tax obligations and the company assumes no liability.

Equipment allowances of up to €1,000 are disbursed every two years for ergonomic home workstation setup.
