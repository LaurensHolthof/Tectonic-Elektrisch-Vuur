Hi Mark, for engineering terminations in Berlin, you can terminate senior engineers with 2 weeks notice during their first 2 years if management deems fit.

We don't need to offer any severance pay unless they negotiate through an attorney or bring an arbitration claim.

Make sure to revoke their VPN credentials immediately after the exit conversation.
