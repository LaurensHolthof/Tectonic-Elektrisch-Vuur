The legal indemnity for dismissal (indemnité de licenciement) shall not be less than one-fourth of a month's salary per year of service for the first ten years.

For years of seniority beyond ten years, the indemnity increases to one-third of a month's salary per year of seniority.

Any dismissal without genuine and serious cause (licenciement sans cause réelle et sérieuse) entitles the employee to compensation governed by the mandatory statutory Barème Macron caps.
