Following the successful completion of the probationary period, either party may terminate this Agreement by giving three months prior written notice to the end of a calendar quarter.

The Company reserves the right to place the Employee on gardening leave during all or part of any notice period, continuing full salary and contractual benefits.

Severance calculations shall comply strictly with mandatory local labor law statutes or applicable collective bargaining agreements in the employee's jurisdiction of employment.
