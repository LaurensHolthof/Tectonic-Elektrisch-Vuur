Performance conversations should begin with clear examples, the expected standard, and an opportunity for the employee to share relevant context.

Managers record quarterly goals in the People Portal and revisit them when priorities, staffing, or customer commitments materially change.

A written improvement plan should define no more than three measurable outcomes, the support available, and a review period normally lasting 30 to 60 days.

People Operations must review an improvement plan before it is issued to confirm that expectations are consistent and any accommodation needs have been considered.

Successful completion closes the plan in writing, while an unsuccessful plan may lead to reassignment, a further review period, or a formal employment decision.

Performance ratings must not be lowered because an employee used protected leave, requested an accommodation, raised a concern, or declined work outside agreed hours.
