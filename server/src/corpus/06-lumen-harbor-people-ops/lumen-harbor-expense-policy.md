Business expenses must have a clear company purpose and should be submitted through Spendlight within 30 days of purchase.

Receipts are required for individual expenses above €25, while missing receipts require a written explanation and manager approval.

Standard-class rail is preferred for journeys under five hours, and economy airfare is required for flights under six hours unless an accommodation applies.

Employees may claim up to €450 for home-office equipment every two years after manager approval, but monitors and chairs remain company property.

Client entertainment above €150 per attendee requires advance approval from the relevant vice president and may not include cash gifts.

Finance reviews unusual claims with the employee before rejecting or recovering an expense.
