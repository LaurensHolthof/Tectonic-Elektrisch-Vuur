The hiring manager confirms the start date, work location, job title, reporting line, and equipment needs at least ten working days before arrival.

People Operations sends the employment documents, payroll forms, handbook acknowledgement, and first-week schedule through the secure onboarding portal.

Information Security provides a managed laptop, multifactor authentication, role-based system access, and security training before production access is granted.

The manager assigns an onboarding partner and schedules introductions, product training, and a 30-day expectations conversation.

New employees should not send identity documents, bank details, or medical information through ordinary email or team chat.

The checklist is complete only after the employee and manager confirm that required access, training, and payroll details are correct.
