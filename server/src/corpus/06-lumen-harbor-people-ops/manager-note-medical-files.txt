The team is considering whether to upload medical documentation to the shared manager folder so coverage decisions can be made quickly.

The folder is currently accessible to all department managers and is also used for holiday schedules and staffing plans.

Before any documents are collected, the manager will ask People Operations to confirm the approved confidential storage process and the minimum information needed for scheduling.
