Employees may work from home up to three days per week when their role and team commitments support a hybrid arrangement.

Each team publishes two anchor days per month for planning, workshops, or customer sessions that benefit from in-person attendance.

Temporary work from another country is limited to 20 working days per calendar year and requires an approved International Work Request before travel is booked.

People Operations, Payroll, Information Security, and the employee's manager review international requests for tax, social security, immigration, and data-access risks.

Employees must use company-managed devices and the corporate VPN when accessing confidential information away from a Lumen Harbor office.

An approved remote arrangement may be reviewed when the employee changes role, moves country, or experiences sustained performance or security issues.
