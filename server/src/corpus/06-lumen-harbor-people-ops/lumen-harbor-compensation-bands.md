Every role is assigned a career level and local salary band based on scope, decision-making, expertise, and market data rather than current salary history.

Employees below the midpoint may progress more quickly within a band as they demonstrate consistent performance at level.

Promotion requires sustained work at the next level, a documented business need, and calibration with peer roles across the company.

The annual compensation review considers market movement, internal equity, performance, and the budget approved by the board.

Managers may discuss career expectations but must not promise a specific promotion, salary increase, equity grant, or review outcome before calibration is complete.

People Operations performs an annual pay-equity review and escalates unexplained differences for correction.
