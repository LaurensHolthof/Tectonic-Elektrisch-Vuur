Annual leave should be requested in the People Portal before travel is booked, except when an emergency makes advance notice impractical.

Managers should respond to ordinary leave requests within five working days and may decline only for a documented business coverage reason.

Employees may carry five unused annual leave days into the next calendar year, and carried days should normally be used before 31 March.

Lumen Harbor provides five paid wellbeing days each year for short-term illness, preventive care, or urgent family needs without requiring a medical certificate.

Longer sickness, parental leave, bereavement leave, and caregiver leave are coordinated with People Operations under the employee's local country policy.

No employee is expected to monitor messages or remain available during approved leave.
