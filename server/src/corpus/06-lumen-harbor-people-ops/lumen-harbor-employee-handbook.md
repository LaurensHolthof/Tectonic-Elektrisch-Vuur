Lumen Harbor Technologies is a fictional renewable-energy software company with teams in Brussels, Berlin, London, and Lisbon.

The standard working week is 38 hours, and employees may arrange their daily schedule with their manager around shared collaboration hours from 10:00 to 15:00 local time.

Employees receive 25 days of annual leave in addition to locally observed public holidays, with requests submitted through the People Portal.

Managers are expected to discuss workload, development goals, and wellbeing during monthly one-to-one meetings rather than waiting for the annual review cycle.

Concerns about conduct, safety, payroll, or workplace treatment may be raised with a manager, People Operations, or the confidential Speak Up channel without retaliation.

Country-specific policies and employment agreements take precedence whenever they provide a different or more protective benefit.
