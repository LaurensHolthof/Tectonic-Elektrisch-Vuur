title: Lumen Harbor Employee Offboarding Checklist
sourceType: internal_memo
date: 2026-03-18
jurisdiction: Global
language: en
register: corporate_standard
---

People Operations confirms the final working date, notice arrangements, final pay inputs, benefits information, and the employee communication plan.

The manager transfers active work and records access needs without asking the departing employee to disclose confidential personal information.

Information Security schedules account changes for the agreed departure time and preserves business records under the retention policy.

The employee returns company equipment using a prepaid service and receives a written inventory confirmation.

Payroll provides the final payslip and any required country documents through the secure employee portal.

Access must not be removed before the agreed time unless People Operations and Information Security document a specific safety or security reason.
