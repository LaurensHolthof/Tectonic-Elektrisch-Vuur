The proposed structure creates separate Forecasting and Market Integrations squads while retaining one shared design and data platform practice.

No roles are currently proposed for elimination, and reporting-line changes remain drafts until leadership and People Operations complete the review.

Managers should not discuss individual placement assumptions before the affected employees receive the same confirmed structure and timeline.

Elena Voss is proposed as design lead across both squads, subject to workload review and her input on the scope.

The communication plan includes a manager briefing, an all-team meeting, individual follow-ups, and a written frequently asked questions page.

Questions about title, compensation, location, or accommodations should be logged for People Operations rather than answered speculatively.
