Employees may request a workplace accommodation verbally or in writing from People Operations without disclosing a diagnosis to their manager.

People Operations discusses the work-related limitation, possible adjustments, expected duration, and any essential role requirements with the employee.

Medical documentation is requested only when necessary to understand the functional limitation and is stored in a restricted case-management system separate from the personnel file.

Managers receive only the information needed to implement an approved adjustment and must not ask colleagues to evaluate whether the accommodation is justified.

Temporary measures may be introduced while the interactive review is underway, including schedule flexibility, remote work, equipment, or task adjustments.

An employee may ask People Operations to reconsider a denied or ineffective accommodation at any time.
