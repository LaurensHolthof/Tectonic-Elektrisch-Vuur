Section 1: Purpose, Scope, and Expanded Workation Allowance
Following our global organizational mobility review, this Policy supersedes all prior iterations of the Remote Work and Telecommuting Guidelines (including v1.2 and v2.0). Eligible employees may now work remotely from an approved international location for up to thirty (30) working days (six calendar weeks) per rolling 12-month period. These days may be taken as a single block or split into multiple journeys, providing employees with substantial flexibility to spend time with family abroad or work across borders while maintaining operational momentum.

Section 2: Revised Eligibility Criteria
All permanent full-time and regular part-time personnel are eligible to participate in the International Workation program immediately upon successful completion of their statutory or contractual probationary period (standard 3 months). The prior requirement for twelve (12) months of continuous tenure is formally abolished. Employees in good performance standing require no special tenure milestones beyond probation clearance.

Section 3: Streamlined Application Procedure and Notice Period
International workation requests must be submitted directly through the Self-Service HR Portal (Workday) under the "International Mobility & Workation" workflow at least fourteen (14) calendar days prior to the intended start date of the travel. This replaces the former 60-day notice requirement. The request requires approval exclusively from the employee's immediate Line Manager. Once the Line Manager approves the schedule and timezone continuity plan, People Operations automatically conducts an asynchronous statutory compliance check within five (5) business days. Vice President (VP) or executive escalation is no longer required for standard workation submissions.

Section 4: Comprehensive Corporate Travel and Emergency Health Coverage
All approved workations are automatically covered under the company’s Global Business Travel and Emergency Medical Assistance insurance program (underwritten by Allianz Global Corporate Specialty / International SOS). This coverage protects the employee for the entire duration of the approved working days abroad at zero cost to the employee, encompassing emergency inpatient and outpatient medical care, emergency dental relief, prescription medications, evacuation, and 24/7 medical concierge support. Employees are not required to purchase independent private health insurance for the working portion of their trip. Any extended personal holiday days taken immediately before or after the 30-day workation window must be covered by personal travel insurance.

Section 5: Eligible Jurisdictions and Immigration Compliance
Workations are authorized across more than 45 approved countries listed in Appendix A, including all European Union (EU) and European Economic Area (EEA) member states, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and select bilateral treaty partner nations. Employees must hold established legal citizenship or existing valid, unrestricted right-to-work status in the destination country (e.g., EU passport holders working across EU member states); the company will not sponsor immigration visas or temporary working permits for voluntary workation arrangements.
