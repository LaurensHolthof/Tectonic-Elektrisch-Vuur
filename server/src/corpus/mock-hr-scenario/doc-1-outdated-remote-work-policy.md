Section 1: Scope and Strict Annual Allowance
All international telecommuting and remote work outside the employee's country of contractual employment is strictly capped at a maximum of 10 business days per calendar year. Under no circumstances may an employee exceed 10 business days of working from abroad in any single 12-month period, as cross-border activity beyond this limit was historically evaluated as posing heightened corporate permanent establishment exposure. Unused international days cannot be accrued or rolled over into subsequent calendar years.

Section 2: Eligibility and Minimum Tenure
Eligibility for international remote work is strictly restricted to regular full-time employees who have attained at least twelve (12) consecutive months of continuous tenure with the enterprise. Employees currently serving a probationary period, or those with less than one full year of service, are strictly prohibited from requesting or undertaking international telework.

Section 3: Advance Notification and Dual-Level Executive Approval Workflow
To initiate an international telecommuting request, the employee must lodge a formal written application via the legacy HR ticketing system at least sixty (60) calendar days prior to the proposed travel date. The application must obtain two-tier written executive sign-off: first from the employee’s direct line manager, and subsequently from the Department Vice President (VP) and the Head of Corporate Risk. Verbal agreements, Slack messages, or informal manager approvals are legally invalid and may result in disciplinary action.

Section 4: Mandatory Private Health Insurance Procurement
The enterprise does not extend corporate travel insurance, health coverage, or medical indemnity to employees engaged in voluntary international remote work. Employees are solely responsible for procuring private, comprehensive international health and emergency repatriation insurance covering their entire duration abroad, and must furnish verified proof of coverage to the People Operations team at least 14 days before departure.

Section 5: Geographic Restrictions and Right to Work
International telecommuting is permissible solely within the territories of the European Economic Area (EEA). All travel to non-EEA destinations, third-party jurisdictions, or countries with differing bilateral social security treaties is entirely disallowed under this policy version.
