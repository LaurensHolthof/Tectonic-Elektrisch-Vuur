Any employee claiming parental leave must notify the employer in writing no later than seven weeks before the leave begins, provided the leave is taken for the period up to the child's third birthday.

For parental leave between the child's third and eighth birthday, the notification period to the employer shall be thirteen weeks before the commencement of the intended leave period.

During the statutory parental leave period, the employer may not terminate the employment relationship from the time the parental leave is requested, but at the earliest eight weeks before the start of parental leave.
