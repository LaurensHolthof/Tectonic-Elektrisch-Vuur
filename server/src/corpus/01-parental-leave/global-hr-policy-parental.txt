All full-time employees are entitled to company parental leave benefits following the completion of their six-month probationary period.

Employees must give 12 weeks written notice prior to commencing parental leave to ensure adequate operational handover and project continuity.

Eligible employees taking primary caregiver leave receive 16 weeks of 100% base salary replacement, running concurrently with any state or statutory allowances.
